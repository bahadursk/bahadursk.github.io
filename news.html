News & Updates

What is happening at AURA Lab.

A running record of new publications, fellowships, appointments, talks, and milestones from the group. Press and collaboration enquiries are welcome.

Nov 2026

Open online book: Molecules, Data and Python

The interactive Jupyter Book version 1.0 is now free to read online. It teaches Python through chemistry from school level to research level and applies machine learning across chemistry and materials science. Text CC BY 4.0, archived on Zenodo (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24583664).

Nov 2026

Book: Molecules, Data and Python

Dr. Bahadur Sk’s book Molecules, Data and Python: Your First Steps in Chemistry Coding is published by Notion Press — a first course in Python for school students, taught through the chemistry they already study.

Oct 2026

Third PhD scholar joins AURA Lab

Ramesh Babu Kota joins the group as a PhD research scholar.

Sep 2026

Review in ACS Appl. Mater. Interfaces

BODIPY-Integrated Porous Materials: Design Principles, Functional Architectures, and Emerging Applications is published in ACS Applied Materials & Interfaces.

2026

New M.Sc. course sequence in AI-driven chemistry

The first of two newly designed courses, Computational Thinking and Data Science for Chemists, opens for M.Sc. Chemistry students, followed by Cheminformatics and Machine Learning for Molecules in the even semester. See Teaching.

Jul 2026

First MSc thesis students join AURA Lab

Dasari Nagaambika and Pasupuleti Akhila join the group as MSc thesis students, working on excited-state dynamics and room-temperature phosphorescent materials for bioimaging.

2026

J. Mater. Chem. C paper on flexible delayed-fluorescence emitters

Design Strategy for Inducing Delayed Fluorescence via Molecular Flexibility is published in J. Mater. Chem. C (2026, 14, 14527–14534), alongside a ChemRxiv preprint on sub-microsecond delayed fluorescence in triazatruxene emitters for OLEDs.

Jan 2026

First PhD scholars join AURA Lab

Kalingi LVSCS Rao and Sudharani Bheemanathini join as the group’s first PhD research scholars, working on AI-guided design of afterglow materials and hyperphosphorescent OLED emitters.

2025

ANRF National Postdoctoral Fellowship and KLU seed grant

Awarded the ANRF National Postdoctoral Fellowship (2025) and, at KL University, an institutional seed research grant (2025–2026) to build computational infrastructure for AI-assisted materials design.

Aug 2025

AURA Lab opens at KL University (Deemed to be University)

Dr. Bahadur Sk joins the Department of Chemistry as Assistant Professor and establishes AURA Lab (Advanced Unimolecular Radiative Afterglow), beginning recruitment for PhD and project students.

Apr 2025

New paper in J. Phys. Chem. Lett.

Synchronizing the nπ* Transition Dipole in a Benzoyl-Linked N-Fused Ring for Highly Efficient Yellow Afterglow appears in J. Phys. Chem. Lett. — first authorship from the AURA Lab principal investigator.

2025

Adv. Mater. publication on through-bond interactions

Collaborative work on selective lower-occupied through-bond interactions for efficient organic phosphorescence enabling high-resolution long-wavelength afterglow is published in Advanced Materials.

2025

J. Mater. Chem. C study on TADF emitter optimisation

New paper on donor linkage and acceptor nitrogen-atom position optimisation in TADF emitters, improving external quantum efficiency and efficiency roll-off in OLEDs.

Feb 2025

Senior Research Associate at IACS Kolkata

Joined the DST Technical Research Centre at the Indian Association for the Cultivation of Science (Kolkata) as Senior Research Associate, leading computational materials design for organic field-effect transistors and high-resolution imaging probes.

Sep 2024

Oral talk at the Annual Meeting on Photochemistry 2024

Presented “Synchronized Twosome in Benzoyl Linked N-Fused Ring for Highly Efficient Yellow Afterglow” at the Japanese Photochemistry Association meeting, Kyushu University.

Oct 2023

Anti-counterfeit afterglow paper

Collaborative paper on continuous condensed triplet accumulation for irradiance-induced anti-counterfeit afterglow appears in Advanced Science.

Jul 2023

Contributed talk at ICP 2023

Presented work on symmetry-breaking triplet excited states for enhanced red afterglow at the 31st International Conference on Photochemistry, Hokkaido, Japan.

2023

Adv. Funct. Mater. paper on triplet-singlet FRET for bright red afterglow

New work on selective triplet–singlet Förster-resonance energy transfer for bright red afterglow emission appears in Advanced Functional Materials.

Apr 2022

UEC Postdoctoral Fellowship begins

Began the UEC Postdoctoral Fellowship at the University of Electro-Communications, Tokyo — with Prof. Shuzo Hirata, working on theory-guided design of organic afterglow materials.

Jan 2021

C. V. Raman Postdoctoral Fellowship at IISc

Began the C. V. Raman Postdoctoral Fellowship at the Materials Research Centre, IISc Bengaluru, with Dr. P. Rajamalli, working on TADF emitters and OLED devices.

Dec 2020

PhD research completed at IISER Bhopal

Completed doctoral research with Prof. Abhijit Patra on multichromophoric organic systems, solid-state emission, and long-lived emissive materials at IISER Bhopal.
